## 🗺️ Suggested Routes

### 【Must Do】Pick 2-3
- [ ] Senso-ji + Asakusa morning → arrive by 8am to avoid crowds
- [ ] Shibuya Crossing + Harajuku → afternoon to evening
- [ ] Tsukiji Outer Market → breakfast/sushi morning

### 【Recommended】Pick 3-5
- [ ] Meiji Shrine + Yoyogi Park (morning, peaceful)

## 💡 Tips & Warnings

### ✅ Do
1. Get a Suica/Pasmo IC card immediately
2. Trains stop at midnight — plan accordingly
3. Tipping is NOT customary
4. Learn basic Japanese phrases (arigato, sumimasen)

### ❌ Don't
1. Don't eat while walking (rude)
2. Don't talk loudly on trains
3. Don't tip (confusing for staff)
4. Don't assume everyone speaks English
